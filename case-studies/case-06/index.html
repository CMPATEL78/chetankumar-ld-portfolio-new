Designing and facilitating experiential learning for diverse workforce contexts — converting communication, teamwork, professionalism, discipline and leadership concepts into behaviours learners can practise and apply.

01. The Capability Challenge

My corporate L&D experience extends beyond technical and academic learning into human, behavioural and leadership capability development.

I have designed and facilitated learning interventions for employees, trainees, volunteers and leaders across very different environments. The recurring challenge was to turn concepts such as communication, teamwork, discipline, professionalism and leadership into behaviours that people can actually practise.

Design principle: experiential rather than lecture-dominated learning — learners participate, practise, reflect and connect the experience to real behaviour.

02. Silver Consumer Electricals Limited

Corporate capability development across a large manufacturing environment. Silver Consumer Electricals Limited operates a vertically integrated manufacturing campus comprising 9 plants and approximately 4,000 employees. Within this environment, I conducted 5+ workshops involving approximately 300 employees across multiple organisational levels and plants.

Capability areas

  • Leadership
  • Communication
  • Teamwork
  • Workplace behaviour
  • Professionalism
  • Discipline
  • Positive work attitude
  • Role and responsibility

Context-driven delivery

The interventions were adapted according to the learner population and operational context rather than delivered as a fixed programme.

04. Contextual Adaptation

Solar Plant

The core learning objectives were retained, while training duration was reduced, the number of activities/games was reduced, and activities were selected according to the operational context after close liaison with the Plant Head.

Employee Onboarding

The same fundamental capability areas were adapted for new employees: communication, teamwork, professional behaviour, discipline, and roles and responsibilities.

Same learning objective → different learner context → different instructional treatment.

05. Sarangpur Temple — Vyaktigat Vikas Shibir

Professionalism, leadership and seva culture. A different capability-development intervention was conducted at Shri Kashtabhanjan Dev Hanumanji Mandir, Sarangpur. The programme, Vyaktigat Vikas Shibir, was a two-day intensive personal-development workshop for approximately 300 temple sevaks across batches.

Core learning areas

  • Body language and discipline
  • Manager vs. Leader
  • Comfort Zone → Growth Zone
  • Good Follower → Leader
  • Courage, humility and seva-bhav
  • Reframing work as seva/service

Experiential approach

Activities such as Balloon Coordination Walk and Blindfold Zig-Zag Navigation were used to develop teamwork, coordination, trust, communication and leadership responsibility.

Contextual adaptation: conceptual clarity + reflective questioning + experiential activities + spiritual anchoring, while respecting the cultural context of the volunteer environment.

10. Portfolio Takeaway

I design behavioural learning as an experience, not a presentation.

My professional training work has involved very different learners and environments — from manufacturing employees and new joiners to plant teams, leaders and temple sevaks. I adapt the depth, language, activities, duration and facilitation approach to the learner and context while retaining the intended capability outcome.

Awareness→Practice→Reflection→Workplace Behaviour

This case complements the earlier instructional-design and learning-system cases by demonstrating the human and behavioural side of my L&D practice.
