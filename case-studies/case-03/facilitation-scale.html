10 — Multi-Modal Facilitation & Scale

The programme used several learning formats:

Presentation→Live AI Demonstration→Individual Hands-on Practice→Group Activity→Business Scenario→Output Review→Participant Presentation

This was particularly important for a large, mixed-seniority audience. The programme was therefore designed as a participatory learning experience, not a passive technology briefing.

Seven plants

Designed for leadership-positioned employees across seven plants.

~75 participants/session

Required the design to work effectively at scale.

Individual experimentation

Participants worked on their own devices.

Group collaboration

Collaborative challenges created observable application.
